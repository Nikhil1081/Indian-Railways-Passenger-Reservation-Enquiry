Rules & Policies

Flat cancellation fees on Confirmed Tickets (>48 hrs before train departure):

  • AC First / Executive: ₹240 + GST
  • AC 2 Tier / First Class: ₹200 + GST
  • AC 3 Tier / CC: ₹180 + GST
  • Sleeper Class (SL): ₹120
  • Second Class (2S): ₹60

No refund is granted on confirmed Tatkal tickets cancelled after chart preparation.

Tatkal reservation timings open one day in advance of journey date:

  • AC Classes (1A/2A/3A/CC): Opens at 10:00 AM daily.
  • Non-AC Classes (SL/2S): Opens at 11:00 AM daily.

Maximum of 4 passengers per booking is permitted on a single Tatkal request.

Allowed only once by submitting a request to the Chief Reservation Supervisor 24 hours prior to departure:

  • Transfers only allowed to immediate family members (Spouse, Parents, Children, Siblings).
  • Government employees can transfer to other colleagues on duty.

Catering is optional on Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Duronto trains. In case you opt out during booking, you cannot demand food onboard unless extra billing is done subject to food availability.
